# Purpose
Use to secure the business legally and operationally to the founder.

# Core protections
- Form the correct entity early and separate personal and business finances.
- File federal trademark applications strategically; LLC registration alone is not trademark protection.
- Use founder IP assignment, contractor IP assignment, and work-for-hire language.
- Control domain, DNS, storefront accounts, payment processors, and registrar access personally or through a founder-controlled admin entity.
- Use operating agreement terms, manager authority rules, and approval thresholds.
- Protect cap table, vesting, buyback rights, and transfer restrictions.
