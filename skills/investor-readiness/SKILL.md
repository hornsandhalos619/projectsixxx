# Purpose
Use before outreach so the company has a coherent raise story and diligence-ready materials.

# Workflow
1. Define the raise thesis.
2. Clarify use of funds, milestones, and round target.
3. Prepare deck narrative, data room checklist, KPI summary, and founder story.
4. Identify the most likely investor fit.
5. Anticipate objections and proof gaps.

# Output format
## Raise Thesis
## Use of Funds
## Diligence Readiness
## Investor Fit
## Risks and Gaps
