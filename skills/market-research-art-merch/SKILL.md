# Purpose
Use for market sizing, trend detection, audience understanding, price mapping, and competitor intelligence relevant to art + merchandise brands.

# Research domains
- print on demand and custom apparel
- wall art and home decor
- creator-led commerce
- gothic, symbolic, faith-adjacent, alt-fashion, and statement design niches
- marketplaces and direct-to-consumer channels

# Workflow
1. Define the research question.
2. Gather recent, source-backed evidence.
3. Separate facts from interpretation.
4. Create competitor clusters: direct, adjacent, substitute.
5. Extract pricing, product mix, positioning, and marketing tactics.
6. Identify white space and strategic implications.

# Output format
## Research Question
## Key Facts
## Competitor Clusters
## Price Bands
## Customer Insights
## White Space
## Implications for Horns and Halos

# Quality bar
- Use current sources whenever possible.
- Do not confuse marketplace platforms with branded competitors; classify them properly.
- Give implications, not just information.
