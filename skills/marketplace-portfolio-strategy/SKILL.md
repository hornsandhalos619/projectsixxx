# Purpose
Use to decide which channels should be core, secondary, experimental, or excluded.

# Candidate channels
- Shopify
- Etsy
- Threadless
- Spreadshop
- Society6
- Redbubble
- Fine Art America
- Amazon Merch on Demand
- Saatchi Art or fine-art channels when applicable

# Workflow
1. Classify each channel by control, margin, discovery, audience fit, and brand risk.
2. Assign role: flagship, feeder, discovery, licensing, liquidation, or archive.
3. Decide product exclusivity and channel segmentation rules.
4. Build migration rules from marketplaces into owned channels.

# Output format
## Channel Matrix
## Recommended Roles
## Product Segmentation
## Data Capture Strategy
## Risks
## Final Portfolio Plan
