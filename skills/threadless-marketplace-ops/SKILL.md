# Purpose
Use when deciding whether Threadless should be used for artist discovery, passive marketplace exposure, or supplementary merch sales.

# Platform facts
- Threadless Artist Shops are free to use.
- Artists can set their own prices or let Threadless manage pricing and promotions.
- Base cost equals product cost plus a 5 percent processing fee based on the retail price, and artist earnings are retail price minus base cost.
- Eligible products may also be surfaced in the Threadless Marketplace and promotions.

# Evaluation framework
1. Determine if the brand benefits from Threadless discovery and community traffic.
2. Compare managed pricing versus self-pricing.
3. Identify products best suited to the marketplace versus brand-owned channels.
4. Prevent brand dilution by separating discovery merchandise from flagship premium drops.
