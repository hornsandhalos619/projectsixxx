# Purpose
Use for deciding what to sell, in what order, at what depth, and with what launch cadence.

# Workflow
1. Split products into evergreen, seasonal, limited, and collab categories.
2. Rank by brand fit, demand potential, margin, complexity, and operational risk.
3. Build launch capsules with hero products first.
4. Define what to test in POD versus what to reserve for bulk or premium runs.
5. Establish re-order and kill criteria.

## Next Drops
