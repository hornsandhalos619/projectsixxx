
# Marketplace Strategy Notes

## Core principle
Use Shopify as the flagship brand home. Use marketplaces for discovery, cash-flow support, lower-risk testing, and audience capture.

## Spreadshop
Best used as a low-friction hosted merch outpost or supplemental channel for evergreen products and creator-audience merchandise.

## Threadless
Best used as an artist discovery and passive marketplace channel for graphic-led products that can benefit from internal marketplace exposure.

## Other appropriate channels
- Etsy for search-driven buyers and gift traffic.
- Society6 for art and home decor adjacency.
- Redbubble for broad discovery and design-volume testing.
- Fine Art America for wall-art oriented buyers.
- Amazon Merch on Demand for scale experiments where fit exists.

## Guardrails
- Do not put flagship premium drops everywhere.
- Keep channel-specific product segmentation.
- Use inserts, packaging, and owned-channel CTAs wherever policy allows.
