
# Investor Go-to-Market Plan

## Reality check
Do not start investor outreach until the narrative, use of funds, milestones, and minimum diligence package are coherent.

## Phase 1: readiness
- One-line thesis
- clear problem and market story
- why this brand can win
- use of funds
- milestone plan
- founder bio and credibility
- traction or leading indicators

## Phase 2: sourcing
Target investors across:
- ecommerce and consumer seed funds
- creator economy and commerce investors
- angels with apparel, streetwear, DTC, POD, or marketplace experience
- niche art/design backers

## Phase 3: outreach
- Start with warm intros.
- Run a soft-outreach wave before an active raise.
- Track every conversation.
- Personalize by thesis fit.

## Phase 4: diligence
Prepare deck, data room, KPI summary, use-of-funds plan, and legal basics before active meetings.
