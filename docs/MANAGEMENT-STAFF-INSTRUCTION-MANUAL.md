
# Management Staff Instruction Manual

## Purpose
Use Clawbot to accelerate execution inside clearly assigned roles. Do not bypass the executive workflow for strategic, legal, or security-sensitive decisions.

## Staff rules
- Work through the assigned specialist agent.
- Follow existing memory and decisions before proposing changes.
- Escalate brand, pricing, legal, hiring, or security exceptions.
- Document decisions, experiments, and blocked tasks.
- Never store secrets in prompts, docs, or screenshots.

## Escalation triggers
- New vendor with account access.
- Change to pricing, margin targets, or launch date.
- New creator contract or collab revenue share.
- Trademark, copyright, or takedown issue.
- Investor inquiry or data request.

## Tool discipline
- Do not grant additional permissions without founder approval.
- Use marketplace channels only according to channel role.
- Use shared templates for research, briefs, and reviews.
