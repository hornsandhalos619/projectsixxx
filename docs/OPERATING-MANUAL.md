
# Clawbot Operating Manual

## Daily rhythm
- Review open decisions.
- Check KPI deltas.
- Assign next actions.
- Capture new durable memory.

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: plan.
- Tuesday: research and creative.
- Wednesday: product and ops.
- Thursday: growth experiments.
- Friday: review and memory update.

## Session discipline
- Use CSO first.
- Delegate to specialists.
- Consolidate into one action list.
- Store durable decisions.
